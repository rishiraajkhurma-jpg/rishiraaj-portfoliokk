Product case for Blinkit, independent. May 2026 to now.

BlinkBot: a value-exchange basket optimizer

₹9 Cr extra GMV a month, projected across 4.5L daily orders

  • Found the core drop-off trigger: “checkout shock”, a sudden 25 to 30% fee jump that breaks impulse buying and drives cart abandonment.
  • Defined the persona (Aarav, 22, tier 1 city) and mapped the psychology: users don't reject the fee, they reject feeling penalized with no perceived value.
  • Designed BlinkBot, an agentic GenAI flow in three LLM steps (context scan, dynamic bundling, generative nudge) that turns the delivery fee into a personalized value exchange in real time.
  • Projected 20% less cart abandonment, ₹35 AOV lift per order and 20% better D30 repeat purchase.
